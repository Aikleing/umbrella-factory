About Us

Xiamen Aikeling Import & Export Co., Ltd is located in Xiamen, Fujian Province, China.

1. We have been responsible for professional OEM and ODM projects. We specialize in producing various styles of umbrellas, including but not limited to: folding umbrellas, straight umbrellas, golf umbrellas, children umbrellas, etc. With 15 years of experience as a professional umbrella OEM and ODM supplier, we have passed BSCI, AUV, REACH test and Sedex audit.

2. Many styles can be ordered at any time with no minimum order quantity. We are available online for small wholesalers, distributors and low volume sellers.

3. We have the most competitive quotes from our factories. We are committed to providing superior quality and competitive products to our valued customers around the world.
